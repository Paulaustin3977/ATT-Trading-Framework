# Risk Management Standard

Status: Draft ATOS v1.1 governance document

## Risk Categories

- Critical: could invalidate research, breach scope or cause severe operational/security harm.
- High: could materially reduce reliability, auditability or project continuity.
- Medium: could create local inefficiency, ambiguity or maintainability issues.
- Low: minor issue with limited impact.

## Required Risk Fields

- Risk ID.
- Description.
- Severity.
- Owner.
- Evidence/source.
- Mitigation.
- Review date.
- Status: open, mitigated, accepted, transferred or closed.

## Non-Negotiable Critical Risks

- Live-trading or broker-connectivity scope creep.
- Repainting/lookahead defects in released logic.
- Overfitting presented as robust evidence.
- Security leakage of credentials or private repository access.
