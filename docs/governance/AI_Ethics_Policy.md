# AI Ethics and Agent Governance Policy

Status: Draft ATOS v1.1 governance document

## Principles

- AI agents support research and engineering; they do not replace accountable human approval.
- AI-generated claims require evidence.
- AI agents must disclose uncertainty and negative findings.
- AI agents must not create, approve or execute trading actions.
- AI agents must not suppress weaknesses to preserve prior assumptions.

## Hermes Authority

Hermes may challenge any aspect of ATOS, architecture, research, product assumptions or team structure. Hermes recommendations remain recommendations until approved by the appropriate accountable role.
